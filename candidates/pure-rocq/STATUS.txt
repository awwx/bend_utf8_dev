2026-11-12 (WasmCert decoder improvement pass; ../wasmcert-coq/decoder/IMPROVEMENT.txt)
  - Utf8StepFacts.v (new, 188 lines, 16 lemmas, no axioms): the decoder-side pure lemmas
    moved here.  The output bound is now stated in UTF-16 code units (text_units,
    pending_units: decode_units_bound says units out + pending' <= |bytes| + pending);
    the decoder converts units to bytes.  check.sh prints Print Assumptions for 10 of its
    lemmas: 27 "Closed under the global context" in all (was 17).
  - `Global Opaque seq_cp` at the end of Utf8Proofs.v.  seq_cp s = U32_of_N (seq_value s)
    is a record of 32 N.testbit's; downstream must use seq_cp_value / seq_cp_value1..4 /
    seq_cp_bound.  Without it the kernel unfolded seq_cp in the decoder's Qeds (> 60 s);
    this replaces the decoder's per-file Local Strategy.  No proof in this directory
    after that line, so nothing here is affected.
  - Utf8Bridge.v: 11.1 s from scratch (the ~9 s quoted above was with a warm
    .lia.cache).  In vs_form, table_arith (5 x 5 table branches, lia discarding each
    impossible one) takes 0.72 s and the final lia with N subtraction 0.17 s, so N
    subtraction is not the main cost.  New lemma lead_cases (the 9 lead-byte classes
    that occur) used by table_arith: 11.1 s -> 9.9 s.  No statement changed.
    encoding_form (~1.9 s) destructs the 32-boolean record and splits on the shape bits.
  - Utf8Laws.v untouched (byte-identical to the spec).
