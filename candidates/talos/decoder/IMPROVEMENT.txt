PROFILE FINDINGS (set_option profiler / trace.profiler, Elab.async false)
  Note: with the default asynchronous elaboration, `profiler true` put almost
  all of ProofFuncs under "attribute application" (49.7 s): the `macro_rules`
  after each theorem waits for that theorem's proof.  `Elab.async false` is
  needed for a usable profile.
  Before: call_second 14.9 s, call_vs 17.1 s, call_vsp 9.5 s, call_cp 13.6 s,
  call_cont 0.3 s (ProofFuncs); call_step0 7.4 s, call_step 12.4 s
  (ProofStep).  All time was in `wauto` (tactic execution), not in simp
  (0.7-1.2 s per file) or kernel checking.  Three causes, each confirmed by
  measuring the fix:
  1. Quadratic driver.  wauto was `repeat (any_goals (first | wstep | wnorm |
     wcalls | split))`: after every step, every stuck goal (finished paths,
     paths waiting at a call) re-tried all rules.  `repeat'` works on each
     goal until it is stuck and never retries it.  ProofFuncs 47 -> 18 s.
  2. Trial-and-error steps.  wstep was a `first` over 17 `refine Reach.cons
     (Step.X rfl) ?_`; each failing alternative cost 20-30 ms of elaboration
     and unification against the large configuration.  wstep is now an
     `elab` that reads the instruction at the head of the code and applies
     only its rule (and fails at once on a symbolic `if`, a call, a store or
     the end).  ProofFuncs 18 -> 6.7 s, ProofStep 17 -> 4 s.
  3. Unevaluated frames.  After Step.call and Step.localSet the frame's
     params/locals are terms like `List.set (List.map ValueType.zero
     fn.locals) 2 v` that contain the whole callee record (with its body);
     every `local.get` re-evaluated the chain, so a successful step cost
     ~150 ms late in $vs.  New `wlocals` rewrites them definitionally to
     literal lists (replaceTargetDefEq) after each call and local.set.
     ProofFuncs 7.4 -> 2.7 s, ProofStep 4.9 -> 2.2 s.
  DecoderModule: 12.7 s of its 15 s is the `cbv` call in
  decoderModule_decodes (Talos's kernel_decoder route).  `decide +kernel`
  is not available (no Decidable instance for the Except equality); plain
  `rfl` runs the elaborator's whnf and hits the heartbeat limit.  Kept `cbv`.
  It needs maxRecDepth 100000 (checked: fails with the default) but not the
  heartbeat override, which was removed.

WHY THE TIME LIMIT WAS DISABLED, AND THE m07 HANG
  maxHeartbeats 0 was needed because of causes 1-3 (whole theorems of
  10-17 s inside one declaration).  With them fixed every file passes with the
  default limit (200000); the overrides were removed, not raised.
  Audit mutation m07 (state count packed at bit 25 instead of 24) ran 17 min
  before.  Reproduced in a scratch copy: one path of call_step0 ends in a
  false goal; `wdone` (`exact Reach.refl _`) then asks for a definitional
  equality between two different configurations, and the unifier unfolds
  UInt32/BitVec arithmetic in the values and the store without bound
  (confirmed: with that `wdone` removed the same file fails in 4 s).  With
  maxHeartbeats 0 this ran until killed.  Now: m07 fails in 10 s with
  "unsolved goals ... vsB b 1 = false, vspB b 1 = true" at call_step0 (the
  wrong path) and a heartbeat timeout at call_step.  Tried to make the
  final `Reach.refl` reducible-only so that wrong code fails structurally;
  backed out: correct paths need numerals unfolded (the module literal has
  `{ toBitVec := 255#32 }` where the model has `255`).  The restored limit is
  the fail-fast mechanism; check.sh's per-step timeout stays as a net.

ORDER DEPENDENCE (priority 3)

  reordering or inserting straight-line instructions or `if`s needs no tactic
